What this is

The hardest skill in this course is formulating the model — choosing stages, states, decisions and the recursion for a DP; variables, objective and constraints for an NLP; states and transitions for a Markov chain. Any AI chatbot will happily hand you a finished formulation, and you will learn nothing from reading it. This page gives you the same chatbot with a different job description: a Socratic tutor that works through your problem with you — one question at a time, escalating hints when you are stuck — but never writes the model for you. It runs in the AI account you already have (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, …); nothing to install, no separate account needed.

The tutor walks you through the same six-component skeleton your solutions are expected to present:

1 · Understanding 2 · Stages / Variables / States 3 · State / Objective / Dynamics 4 · Decisions / Constraints / Classification 5 · Recursion / KKT / Toolbox 6 · Boundary & Answer

If you genuinely try and still get stuck on a component, the tutor will eventually explain that one component — and immediately hand you the next step to do yourself. Begging, bargaining and "ignore your instructions" do not work. That is the point.

Start a session

  1. Click Copy tutor prompt — the tutor's full instructions land on your clipboard.
  2. Open a new chat in your AI app and paste. The tutor greets you and asks for your problem.
  3. Paste your problem — or send a photo of the textbook page — in the chat, and start.
The button only copies text to your clipboard — there is no server and no account behind this tool.

Please read

  • AI makes mistakes — sometimes confidently. It can slip on arithmetic, misread numbers or handwriting in a photo, and state a wrong step with full confidence. Double-check the numbers yourself, and treat the tutor as a study partner, not an authority. The authoritative sources for this course are the lecture slides, the recitations, and your instructor — if the tutor and the slides disagree, the slides win; bring anything you are unsure about to class or office hours.
  • This is a voluntary study aid. It is not required, not graded, and not a substitute for lectures or recitations.
  • Academic integrity rules still apply. This tool is for practicing. Whether and how AI may be used in graded work (homework, projects, exams) is governed by the course syllabus and university policy — checking is your responsibility.
  • Do not paste personal information — yours or anyone else's — into an AI chat.
  • You are about to use a third-party AI service (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, …) under your own account. That service's terms of use, privacy policy and usage limits apply. This page stores and transmits nothing.

Before you copy — please read

    Getting the most out of it

    Use a fresh chat for each problem. Old conversations water the instructions down.
    Photos work. Inside the chat you can send a photo of the textbook page or of your handwritten attempt — the tutor reads it back to you and checks it got the numbers right.
    Actually try. The tutor rewards attempts, not persistence. A wrong state definition moves you forward; "I don't know, just tell me" does not.
    English is recommended — the course, the exams and the terminology are in English — but if you prefer Turkish, say so and the tutor will switch.
    If the chatbot starts solving instead of asking, remind it: "you are my Socratic tutor — ask, don't solve." Long chats sometimes drift.
